My Career Goals

Short Term

My immediate focus is on building a strong foundation in DevOps and cloud engineering. I recently accepted a Junior DevOps Engineer role where I will gain hands-on professional experience working with cloud infrastructure in a production environment. Alongside my new role I plan to complete my Bachelor of Science in Information Technology at Western Governors University by December 2026, while earning my CompTIA Security+ and AWS Solutions Architect Associate certifications. My goal in the short term is to become a well-rounded engineer who can independently design, deploy, and maintain reliable cloud systems.

Long Term

Over the next several years I aim to grow from a DevOps Engineer into a Site Reliability Engineer and eventually a Cloud Architect. I want to develop deep expertise across infrastructure as code, platform engineering, and distributed systems, taking on increasing ownership and technical leadership along the way. My long-term vision is to reach a Staff or Principal Engineer level where I can shape architecture decisions and mentor other engineers. Ultimately I want to build a career at the intersection of reliability, automation, and cloud-native development while continuing to push my technical boundaries.
